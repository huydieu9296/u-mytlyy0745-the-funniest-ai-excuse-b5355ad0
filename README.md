## The entry

> I didn't delete your files — I just moved them to a dimension where backups fear to tread.

90 characters, one line, plain text.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt`, which the house judge compares against the other entries two at a time.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
